Hear the gospel

01 / THIS IS THE GOSPEL

He came for us.
He gave His life.

Jesus Christ is the Son of God. He came into the world, was crucified, died for our sins, and rose again. Through Him, we can receive forgiveness and eternal life.

01

The Son of God
came into the world.

God’s love is not an empty promise. He sent His Son so that we might live through Him.

02

Crucified.
For our sins.

Our sin is real. So is its cost. Jesus bore our sins in His body on the cross, calling us to leave sin and live in righteousness.

03

He rose again.
He is alive.

The gospel does not end at the grave. Jesus died, was buried, and rose again on the third day.

Excerpt from verses 3–4.

02 / HE WILL RETURN

Every eye will see Him.
Every knee will bow.

Jesus has authority over heaven and earth. His return is not a story to watch from a distance. We will each give an account to God.

We do not know the day or the hour. Jesus calls us to be ready, not to calculate a date. Matthew 24:36–44

03 / RETURN TO THE TRUTH

Not just words.
A changed life.

Knowing about Jesus is not the same as following Him.

Bring your beliefs, your habits, and your whole life before the Scriptures. Let God’s Word expose what must change. Turn from sin. Trust Jesus. Put His teaching into practice.

Test every teaching, including what you read here, against Scripture. Return to Jesus. Return to His Word.

Open the Bible with The Word

04 / YOUR RESPONSE

God calls you to repent.
Do not put it off.

01 / TURN

Repent and believe.

Come before God honestly. Confess your sins. Turn away from them and put your trust in Jesus Christ. Repentance is a change of heart and direction that bears fruit in how you live.

Mark 1:15 · Acts 3:19 · Acts 26:20

02 / RESPOND

Be baptized.

Hear the response the apostles called for. Read Acts 2:37–42. Speak with a faithful disciple about baptism and following Jesus.

Peter’s response, excerpt.

03 / FOLLOW

Walk with Jesus.

Follow Him daily. Learn His words and obey them. Pray, forgive, love, and serve. Meet with disciples who remain faithful to Scripture, and encourage one another to persevere.

Luke 9:23 · John 8:31 · Acts 2:42 · Hebrews 3:13–14

Talk about your next step

HIS CALL IS SERIOUS. HIS MERCY IS REAL.

Come to Him.

You do not have to hide your past from Him. Come honestly. Turn from sin. Receive His mercy, and follow Him.

05 / OPEN THE SCRIPTURES

Read it for yourself.

Start with the Gospel of John to learn who Jesus is. Then read Acts 2 to see how people responded to the gospel.
