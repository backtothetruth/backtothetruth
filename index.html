HEBREWS 3:15 · PARAPHRASE

01 / THE CALL

Stop drifting.
Turn back to God.

We can fill our lives with religion and still refuse to obey God. We can know the language of faith while our hearts remain far from Him. It is time to examine where we stand.

When Jonah warned Nineveh, the people turned from their evil ways, and God showed mercy. Through Jeremiah, God called His people to amend their ways and stop trusting in empty religious assurances. These accounts confront us with a question: will we hear the Word of God and respond?

READ JONAH 3 · JEREMIAH 7:1–11

Bring your beliefs, your habits, and your whole life before the Scriptures. Where they expose sin, repent. Where Jesus commands, obey.

Jesus calls us to repent and believe the gospel. He died for our sins and rose again. His mercy gives us hope, and His lordship calls for our surrender. Do not put off turning to Him.

READ MARK 1:15 · 1 CORINTHIANS 15:3–4 · ACTS 17:30–31

This call begins with us. Every teaching shared here must be tested against Scripture. Open the Bible with us, and hold us accountable to what it says.

03 / YOUR RESPONSE

You have heard the call.
How will you respond?

Do not leave this as another page you read and forget. Open the Scriptures. Come before God honestly. Take the step you know you need to take.

01 / EXAMINE

Open the Word

Read Jeremiah 7:1–11 and Luke 6:46–49. Where have you relied on religious words while resisting what God asks of you?

02 / REPENT

Turn to God

Confess your sins to God. Ask for His mercy. Turn away from what His Word exposes, and put your trust in Jesus Christ.

03 / OBEY

Act on what you hear

Read Acts 2:37–42. Consider the response it describes. If you need to discuss repentance, baptism, or following Jesus, write to us.
